## Introduction

Focusing on the processes of curation and collaboration, the concept of “curated futures” speaks to the possibilities of imagination and speculation. This project creates space for freedom and expression in a wide variety of works that reflect the interests and investments of many collaborators.

## Defining the “Third Library”

The Third Library is imagined as an ethical, socially just, and hope-filled ecosystem of knowledge and cultural production. It challenges what we have come to know about libraries of the past while creating a library for the very near future.

## Afrofuturism and speculative futures

Afrofuturism reimagines time and space through fantasy and technology. Applied to digital humanities and cultural heritage work, it offers a framework for centering communities that have been excluded from world-making and for building practical paths toward decolonial change.

## Introducing the contributions

The collection includes essays, interviews, podcasts, maps, visualizations, games, and immersive experiences. Taken together, these works ask what we want from institutions of the future and how we might create them.
