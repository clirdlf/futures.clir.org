Game-Based Design for Inclusive and Accessible Digital Exhibits

Written by Rebecca Y. Bayeck, Smiti Nathan, and Alex Wermer-Colan

This contribution explores what gaming can offer digital exhibitions, exhibit design, and data curation. It considers design thinking, interactive storytelling, simulated archives, and the audiences traditionally excluded from library exhibits.
