FLAME - Future Libraries, Archives, and Museums in Excavation - A Podcast Series

Written by FLAME project contributors

Open this experience in a new tab ->

FLAME holds the microphone up to archivists, scholars, and museum staff who work with collections pertaining to BIPOC communities. The series explores reinterpretation, community agency, and the future of libraries, archives, and museums.
