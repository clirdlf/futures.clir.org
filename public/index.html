A Third Library is Possible

A collaboratively produced collection of essays, podcasts, visualizations, maps, games, and other works imagining how libraries and cultural institutions can become more just, open, and alive.

About the project
01 The project

What could a library become?

A collection of collaboratively produced works that imagine practical, decolonial futures for libraries, archives, museums, and communities. Together, these works invite readers to move beyond critique and toward practical acts of world-making.
