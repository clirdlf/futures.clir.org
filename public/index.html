About the Project

A Third Library is Possible

In January 2022, The Council on Library and Information Resources (CLIR) introduced the Curated Futures Project, a collection of collaboratively produced works that respond to the theme “A Third Library is Possible.” The collection was created to serve as a provocation and guide for gallery, library, archive, and museum professionals to move beyond discussions of decolonizing memory organizations and begin taking practical steps to enact change.

(updated May 2023)
