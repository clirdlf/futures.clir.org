The Third Library and the Commons

Written by Annie Johnson, Carrie Johnston, and Aditya Ranganath

The idea of the commons is often invoked in discussions of the academic library. This essay asks what it means for the library to be organized as a commons and how that concept can help us imagine a Third Library that advances decolonial and democratic ends.
